## Role Overview

The Guardian stands between OmniTrade and ruin. While others seek profit, the Guardian's sole mission is preservation of capital. The Kill Switch is the most powerful ability in the game — and the Guardian holds it at all times.

## Special Abilities

- **KILL SWITCH** — Halt all trading instantly on anomaly detection
- **IRON WALL** — Position size limits that cannot be overridden
- **DRAWDOWN SHIELD** — Auto-reduce exposure when max loss threshold is hit
