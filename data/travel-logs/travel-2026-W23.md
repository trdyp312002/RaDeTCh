# Week 23, 2026 — Toyama, Japan

**Spot:** Kurobe Gorge Railway (黒部峡谷鉄道)
**Region:** Kurobe, Toyama

Open-air trolley train ride through Japan's deepest gorge, past hot springs and dense forest. Operates spring to autumn.
