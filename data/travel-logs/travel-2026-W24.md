# Week 24, 2026 — Toyama, Japan

**Spot:** Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route (立山黒部アルペンルート)
**Region:** Tateyama, Toyama

Japan's greatest mountain sightseeing route crossing the northern Alps at 2,450m. Snow walls up to 20m in spring.
