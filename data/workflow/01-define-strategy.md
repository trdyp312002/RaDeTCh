## Quest Description

Every great campaign begins with a plan. Before writing a single line of code, the team defines the prey — the market inefficiency that OmniTrade will exploit. Vague strategies die in backtesting. Precise strategies survive.

## Success Criteria

The quest is complete when a written SSD exists that can be independently implemented by any Backend Engineer without ambiguity.
