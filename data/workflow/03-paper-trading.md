## Quest Description

The shadow realm is where illusions are tested. Paper trading reveals what backtesting cannot: real latency, real slippage, real market impact. The bot must survive 2 weeks of continuous operation without failure before entering the arena.

## Success Criteria

- Zero system crashes over 14 consecutive days
- Live paper PnL within 85% of backtest expectations
- Max latency under 200ms on 99th percentile
- All alerts and monitoring systems confirmed working
