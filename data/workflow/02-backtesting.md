## Quest Description

The strategy must prove itself against the past before earning a place in the present. The Backtesting Engine replays years of market history in minutes. Strategies that fail here save real money — losing in simulation is free.

## Minimum Standards to Pass

- Sharpe Ratio ≥ 1.5
- Max Drawdown ≤ 20%
- Win Rate ≥ 45% (or Profit Factor ≥ 1.8)
- Minimum 100 trades in sample
